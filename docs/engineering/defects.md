# Known defects and limitations

What is wrong or unverified in what exists **now**. Planned-but-missing features live in
[todo.md](todo.md). Conventions: [README](README.md).

## Open

| ID | Area | Defect | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| DEF-1 | firmware | Telemetry is a first cut: a node streams its reports to one sink over a dedicated link (no forwarding over ranging contacts, D-6), records carry no age envelope, and the reflector has no estimate of its own. The sink serves up to 4 nodes at once, tested with two. | Every reporting node needs a direct link to the sink. |
| DEF-2 | firmware | The node boots on a TAG and, as the sink, on a DK (crystals start, DC/DC on), but the exact crystal load-cap values (ported from Zephyr, FICR-trimmed) are unverified, and the DK has not run CS. A reflector's 1 s sink link lost sync after its first packet every time with both sides declaring 50 ppm, and held with the sink declaring 250 ppm: a sleep clock on one side is further off than declared. Which one isn't measured. | Frequency offset or RF misbehaviour would show only in an RF test or a CS run; the DK declares 250 ppm meanwhile, which widens its peers' windows a little. |
| DEF-3 | firmware | Antenna 0 is selected outside CS by driving SKY13348 V1 high. NCS leaves both lines low, and the switch truth table has not been checked. | Ordinary BLE traffic could go through the wrong or an isolated path. |
| DEF-4 | firmware | `CS_LINKS` (6) is a guess. `SDC_MEM` is measured on a TAG (95,448 bytes); the one-antenna figure (77,016) is derived, not measured. | A wrong size fails boot with "Memory buffer too small" and the needed size, or warns "too big". |
| DEF-6 | ranging | Phase-slope distances read long on the TAG pair with no board offset calibrated: 2.25 m at a measured 0.6 m with one antenna each (2.85 m, yesterday's and today's firmware alike), 2.2–2.4 m at 10 and 90 cm yesterday, about 1.5 m at 40 cm with 2 × 2 antennas (spike 2026-11-03-four-tags-proximity); the offset differs per antenna path (two good paths 0.3 m apart at 1.25 m); sign and scale check out against hardware and Nordic's estimators. RTT units (0.5 ns) and sign are still unchecked. | Absolute ranges are off by a constant until calibrated. |
| DEF-7 | ranging | Placeholder range σ model `0.05 + 2·residual`. Clean ranges get σ ≈ 0.18 m against a real ≈ 0.005 m. | Solvers under-weight good ranges; in simulation the barometer prior then dominates z (clean `--height baro` p90 0.38 m vs 0.02 m free). |
| DEF-8 | sim | Channel model: one reflector, no amplitude noise, never a blocked direct path. The tracking sim assumes perfect still/moving detection and round-robin node choice. | Simulated numbers are directional only. |
| DEF-9 | solver | The tracker has only a constant-velocity model; one range/s or less while walking loses track. | Walking needs ~2 ranges/s unless optional dead reckoning (FR-T7b) is added. |
| DEF-10 | proto | No telemetry envelope (age, hops). Records carry only origin uptime. | The sink cannot timestamp forwarded records yet (FR-S4). |
| DEF-11 | firmware | The FR-M4 beacon's hop count reads 255 until the mesh state runs on devices (a sink says 0), and pressure isn't measured. The company ID is the SIG's test value 0xFFFF. Only the initiator scans (3 s at boot), and nothing uses what it hears; there is no mesh logic yet. | Tags can't choose nodes or take a height reference from beacons yet. |
| DEF-35 | firmware | A node advertises only while it has no connection: the reflector and the sink stop once a central connects. So its beacon, and a button press's attention flag, are off air while it is linked. | A field tool can't see a linked node's beacon or button press. Multiple advertising sets, with stage 3, fix it. |
| DEF-36 | firmware | The battery reading is the lowest of 16 samples over 1 s, which should land on radio activity, and the thresholds are provisional. Neither has been checked on a coin cell: the bench runs from the DK's 3.3 V. | The status colours and the host's days-to-low estimate may be off until the CR2032 sag measurements. |
| DEF-21 | host | `navgrid cs` keys procedures by counter alone, so a capture spanning a reconnect merges unrelated procedures, and mode-1 RTT values are paired in step order. | Capture one connection per file pair; RTT values are not trusted. |
| DEF-19 | tooling | The DK's J-Link occasionally loses a TAG while CS runs (SWD protocol or AP errors), or answers WAIT. At probe-rs's default 400 kHz it happened within seconds; at 100 kHz (the runner now) once in about 25 runs. Not supply sag: it happens on DK power too. The DKs hang off a USB hub with type-A cables, which may account for some of it. | A capture or flash can fail; rerun it. |
| DEF-20 | deps | TrouBLE `unwrap!`s every LE meta event, so one bt-hci can't parse (e.g. CS capabilities complete v2, subevent 0x38) panics the node. | Don't unmask newer LE events until bt-hci parses them. |
| DEF-22 | deps | embassy-nrf's GRTC time driver hard-codes CC[11], one of the channels nrf-mpsl hands to MPSL (CC[7]–[11]). Moving it to CC[1] didn't change DEF-18, and MPSL wasn't seen using CC[11]. | Latent: MPSL timing could break if it ever uses CC[11]. |
| DEF-27 | mesh | Desired state is not authenticated: any node could write a `Desired` record under the controller's ID. The MAC under a controller key (NFR-7) comes with the fleet key. | Fine on the bench; not for a shared space. |
| DEF-28 | mesh | Every sync session sends a full summary of the classes a node keeps: about 80 % of the bytes, ~650 B per side per session at 500 nodes ([spike](spikes/2026-11-02-mesh-state-sim.md)). | About 250 kB/h sent per node at 60 s sessions; an estimated few µA. |
| DEF-29 | mesh | A stored record takes 136 B of RAM, about three times its wire size, because the node record's eight 64-bit neighbour IDs size the record enum. | A node needs 13–17 KiB at p50 and up to ~34 KiB at busy relays. |
| DEF-30 | mesh | When a node loses its path to every sink, hop counts count up through its peers to `MAX_HOPS` (32) before it reads as unreachable. Pushes refused while they settle (226 in a one-hour partition of 20 nodes). | Records wait a little longer after a partition; nothing is lost. |
| DEF-31 | mesh | Routes come only from records pushed toward sinks, so a command can reach only a node whose records have reached a sink. | A new node can't be commanded until it has pushed once. |
| DEF-32 | deps | TrouBLE runs one security procedure at a time, so a central bringing up several links at once gets `InvalidState`. The firmware retries for up to 5 s. | Links encrypt one after another; untested with more than two. |
| DEF-33 | firmware | The fleet key sits in plain RRAM in the provisioning page, readable over SWD: no APPROTECT, and the KMU is unused. | Anyone with a probe on one device can join the fleet and command it. |
| DEF-34 | deps | TrouBLE still accepts Just Works pairing from any device. A provisioned peripheral drops the link once such a pairing completes, so an outsider holds an encrypted link for a moment, with no channel and no CS. | Small; can't be closed without a TrouBLE option to refuse pairing. |
| DEF-37 | proto | Telemetry frames carry no version of their own: the host decodes only its own `PROTOCOL_VERSION`, and the version in the Hello sits inside the frame it can't read. | After a format change, every device and the host must update together; the old sink's Hello shows as a bad frame. |
| DEF-38 | firmware | A swap installs the whole 720 KiB slot and takes about 25 s, so with the check and reconnect an update stops the node's ranging for about 35 s. (RRAM writes while links run, the other half of this entry, now go in MPSL timeslots.) | Updates stop ranging for half a minute per node. |
| DEF-39 | firmware | An update offer is made once per connection. A peer that declines because its own image isn't confirmed yet, or a transfer cut short by a dropout, waits for the next connection, and the transfer then starts again from byte 0. | A peer can miss an update until its link reconnects; a flaky link may never finish one. |
| DEF-40 | tooling | `navgrid update --probe` writes the update slot through the probe whatever the device's state. On a device whose image isn't confirmed yet, that overwrites the image a revert would restore, and the bootloader would then swap in the staged bytes unchecked. | Bench only: stage onto a confirmed device. |
| DEF-45 | firmware | During an update from the sink, the node's uplink carries about 6.6 frames/s against the 11.1 it produces, so for the 5.5 min of the transfer its outbox stays full and ranges and Health reports are dropped. | The monitor misses ranges and Health while a node takes an update from the sink. |
| DEF-46 | firmware | Twice, the sink's serial receive stopped answering the host partway through an upload (384 of 505 KiB, then 64 of 517 KiB) and stayed silent for every try after, while the sink kept forwarding telemetry; a reset cleared it. Likely in the RRAM path: a page write waits for MPSL timeslots, the host gave up after 5 s against the sink's 10 s write deadline, and clearing the staging page had no deadline at all, so a wedged flash driver would hold the receive loop. Those steps now have deadlines and the host waits 15 s, which turns a stuck write into a decline; the wedge itself isn't explained. | An upload to the sink can fail; a stuck one may still need a reset. |
| DEF-48 | tooling | The build store (`builds/`) keys builds by tag and board. Two dirty builds of one commit, or a build with a test feature (`crash-test`, `never-confirm`), have the same tag, and the later one replaces the earlier: the monitor then decodes the other's frames with the wrong table. | Logs from a device running a dirty or test build can decode wrongly. |
| DEF-49 | firmware | About 6.5 s after an initiator boots and reconnects to the sink, TrouBLE on the initiator logs `error processing signal: NotFound` and `disconnect request for unknown channel` (CID 65): apparently the sink closing a channel from the previous connection on the new one. Seen through the remote logs. | Harmless as far as seen; two warnings per reconnect at warn level. |
| DEF-51 | ranging | The phase-slope estimator has no multipath rejection, and the bench showed it: a board or object within about 0.5 m of a TAG gave scattered or tightly wrong readings; two TAGs flat on the floor 1.5 m apart read 7–10 m, the floor reflection cancelling the direct path; 6.1 m through office partitions read 12–20 m with a quarter of the procedures empty ([spike](spikes/2026-11-03-four-tags-proximity.md)). Clean readings need 1 m of clearance and a line of sight. Two flat pucks at the same low height (chair seats, 0.45 m up) read a bounce whose length barely followed their separation, by phase and by RTT alike: the floor reflection, nearly the direct path's length and inverted, fades the direct path at some distances (two-ray; the antenna pattern is near-uniform); a flat puck on the floor under a node mounted flat at 2.30 m read true + 1.5–2.0 m from 2.3 to 3.8 m, face up or down (spike, late night). | Nodes go high and flat over the floor; tags lie anywhere beneath. Keys under furniture or against a wall are untested, and the estimator still can't tell a bounce from the direct path when it reads one. |
| DEF-52 | tooling | The monitor's days-to-low estimate reports "≈ 0 days" for fresh CR2032s an hour into a session: the cells sag from 3.3 to 2.8 V under radio load in the first hour, and the trend fit takes the sag for the discharge rate. | The battery forecast is wrong until the cells have settled. |
| DEF-53 | ranging | The device's quality figure, the phase fit's residual, is high for wrong answers: in the night series (spike 2026-11-03-four-tags-proximity) every point read 230–246 while the distances were 1.3–4.9 m long and pinned to a reflector. A clean single path that isn't the direct one fits as well as the direct one. | Nothing downstream can tell a reflection's range from a true one; the first-path estimator's component list is the only signal so far. |
| DEF-54 | tooling | `navgrid cs` without `--ipt` multiplies the initiator's tones by the reflector's, but the firmware runs with inline PCT transfer, under which the initiator's tones already carry the round trip and the reflector's are flat: the product is the device's own data, and the day's "host from raw tones" figures (40 cm, evening captures) were not an independent check. The first-path numbers from those runs also had the reflector's flat amplitudes in their weighting. `scripts/cs-capture.sh` passes `--ipt` now. | Re-run the kept captures with `--ipt` before quoting their first-path figures; the phase-slope figures are unchanged. |
| DEF-55 | ranging | The on-device RTT range (`Range` method `Rtt`) is the initiator's ToA−ToD alone: the reflector's turnaround isn't transferred over the air, so each pair reads with its own constant bias (9 m for N005–N004, −2 m for N001–N002 tonight), and the value is reported 100 m up to fit the unsigned field, which the monitor doesn't take off. Access-address-only timing, ±6 m a procedure. | Usable as a per-pair referee for the phase slope's slope, not as a distance. |
| DEF-43 | firmware | The sink's UART receive logs about 36 framing errors per 490 KB upload. The CRC drops the damaged frames and the host resends each after 1 s, so an upload takes 63–77 s against about 50 s clean. Keeping the crystal on didn't help; the likely cause is `read_until_idle` stopping the receiver at gaps in a frame. | Uploads to the sink take about 20 s longer than they need. |
| DEF-25 | ranging | On the TAG pair at one desk placement, antenna path 3 of the 2×2 configuration reads 5–7 m with a phase residual of about 1.3 rad, against 3.3–3.8 m and 0.3–0.4 rad on paths 0–2 ([spike](spikes/2026-11-02-cs-antenna-diversity.md)). Multipath or that antenna pairing; not tested at other placements. | The residual-weighted combination mostly ignores it; the plain mean is off by 0.4 m. |
| DEF-12 | firmware | Building without a board feature prints the intended `compile_error!` plus a follow-on "unresolved import `consts`". | Cosmetic. |

## Fixed

| ID | Defect | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| DEF-44 | The registry recorded only what `navgrid flash` and `navgrid update --probe` wrote, so it missed updates over the air and showed "firmware unknown" for a device flashed before enrolment. | The monitor records the build each device reports in its Hello, Health and Boot, dated, and re-reads the registry when an unknown device reports. |
| DEF-50 | A reflector provisioned with a sink couldn't connect its sink link: the SDC refused `LE Extended Create Connection` (Command Disallowed) after legacy advertising. Then, with beacons on extended PDUs, an initiator's CS link landed anywhere relative to its sink link, and on 5 fresh boots of 5 N001 lost every 4th procedure (8.0/s): connections made from extended advertising don't get the SDC's ACL event spacing (DEF-26). At 120 ms the reflector's own link also sat on 2 procedures in 4. | Beacons advertise through the extended commands with legacy PDUs, so centrals space their links again, and a reflector's sink link runs at 1 s, drifting across the CS procedures. Three fresh boots of N001: 11.0/s with N002 reporting, 14–22 empty procedures a minute, its 1 s link's cost. |
| DEF-47 | The initiator held the central role while it waited, unbounded, to connect to its CS peer. With the peer away its sink link couldn't connect, so it reported nothing; the sink, updated meanwhile, had no node link for 5 min and reverted. | The peer connect gives up after 10 s and yields, so the uplink connects between tries. With N002 away for 2.5 min, N001 kept reporting Health. |
| DEF-42 | On the initiator's sink link, 2M and 251-byte packets let only one exchange into each 2.5 ms event, so the uplink carried at most 8.3 frames/s and the outbox overflowed, even with no update running. | Full-length packets only during an update (`update::listen`), and credits returned four chunks at a time. At rest the uplink is back to 11.1/s with an empty outbox; during an update it carries 6.6/s instead of none (DEF-45). |
| DEF-41 | A sink's update offer to a node over the sink link failed: the transfer stalled near its end, or the node never answered the channel request. The node's telemetry sender, with an outbox kept full by DEF-42, took TrouBLE's per-link send lock again each time it released it, so the update channel's accept and returned credits never went out. | The telemetry sender yields after each frame. The sink then passed a 507 KB update to N001 in 273 s, N001 installed it and passed it to N002 ([spike](spikes/2026-11-03-updates-through-the-sink.md)). |
| DEF-26 | The initiator's sink link collided with its CS subevents. At first, at 100 ms about a third of procedures came back empty, and at 500 ms the link dropped after its supervision timeout. With the CS link up before the sink link, every fourth procedure stayed empty (8.3/s) even with spacing. | ACL event length 2.5 ms, a 120 ms sink interval (a multiple of the CS link's 30 ms), and the central ACL spacing set before each connection. The sink link has to come first, with the CS link 2.5 ms after it, so when the sink link connects under a CS link, the CS link reconnects after it. 11.1/s with no empty procedures in four runs, three of them re-placed (2026-11-03). |
| DEF-5 | `NodeConfig` was a compiled-in default, with no flash storage or provisioning. | A CRC-checked provisioning page in the second-to-last RRAM page, written by `navgrid provision` through the probe, survives firmware flashing. |
| DEF-14 | Role, peer and sink were build-time variables, and links paired Just Works on every connect. | Role, bring-up peer and sink come from provisioning, and links between provisioned devices encrypt under keys derived from the fleet key (D-7), without pairing. |
| DEF-13 | bt-hci 0.11 parsed CS step data column-major; controllers send it row-major. | Fork `DmitriyNE/bt-hci` `2d8d65e`, used by the firmware since the switch to the forks. |
| DEF-15 | Nothing parsed the `CSR`/`CSC` capture lines. | `navgrid cs` (host `capture.rs`) with step decoding in `navgrid-ranging::steps`; checked end to end on simulated captures. |
| DEF-16 | probe-rs 0.32 double-buffered flashing of a TAG (DK onboard J-Link) replaced one word near the start of most pages with its successor; the corrupted images faulted at random places. | Runner passes `--disable-double-buffering` (and `--verify`). Not yet reported upstream. |
| DEF-17 | On the nRF54L15, the SDC hit internal assert `50:701` when a legacy `LE Create Connection` found its peer. The assert's meaning is unknown (Nordic internal); OpenBikeComputer #736 saw the same. | The initiator uses `connect_ext` with `support_ext_scan` / `support_ext_central`. |
| DEF-18 | Every CS subevent on both sides ended aborted with reason 0x3 (scheduling conflict or limited resources), with no steps. Cause: embassy-nrf leaves the nRF54L instruction cache off, and the SDC's per-step CS work can't keep up running from RRAM. | `board::early_init` enables `ICACHE`. 255/255 procedures ranged, no aborts ([spike](spikes/2026-11-02-cs-subevent-aborts.md)). |
| DEF-23 | nrf-mpsl's nRF54L critical section built its reserved-interrupt mask with `=` per NVIC word, so TIMER10 overwrote RADIO_0 and every critical section disabled the radio interrupt. | Fork `DmitriyNE/nrf-sdc` `36c49bc` (ORs the bits), pinned at `6381257`. |
| DEF-24 | Inline PCT transfer gave incoherent initiator tones: the SDC v3.4.0 reflector applied the IPT phase wrongly with several antenna paths (DRGN-29459), and without RRAM standby during radio activity the reflector also missed its phase deadline. | Fork pinned at `6381257`: nrfxlib v3.4.1 and RRAM standby in the low-latency hooks ([spike](spikes/2026-11-02-cs-inline-pct-transfer.md)). |
